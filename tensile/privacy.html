Tensile Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Summary

  • Tensile has no ads and no account or sign-in.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites. The game does not ask for tracking permission and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports with Google Firebase, to balance the levels and fix bugs.
  • Your progress is stored on your device and in your own iCloud account. We do not run our own servers.
  • We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Who we are

Tensile is made by Ilkem Erol, an independent developer who publishes games as AdFree Games. Ilkem Erol is the data controller for the data described here.

Contact: ilkem.erol@hotmail.com

What we collect

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

When you play, the game sends events about gameplay and app use. This includes:

  • a random ID for this installation of the app (the app instance ID). It is not linked to your name, your Apple ID or the advertising identifier;
  • gameplay events, for example a level started, won or lost, a hint used or a rope selected, with the level number;
  • events that the SDK records on its own, for example first open, sessions, screen views and app updates;
  • in-app purchase events (product, price and currency), which the SDK reads from Apple's StoreKit. We never receive your payment details;
  • device and app details: device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • an approximate location (country, region or city). Google derives it from a masked IP address. The game does not use Location Services.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes, it sends a crash report: the stack trace and app state at the time of the crash, the device model, operating system version, whether the device is jailbroken, installation and session IDs, and the last few gameplay events before the crash.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, advertising identifier or payment details.

Data stored on your device and in your iCloud

Your progress, stars, energy, settings, selected rope and purchase status are stored on your device. A copy is kept in the device Keychain, so it comes back when you delete the game and install it again. A copy is also kept in your own iCloud, so it follows your Apple ID to a new iPhone. We cannot see any of these copies.

Apple services

Purchases. Apple handles the optional in-app purchase. Apple's terms and privacy policy apply to the payment.

Game Center (optional). If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your score (your level and stars) to the Game Center leaderboard. Apple runs Game Center and shows your Game Center name and avatar to other players. You can sign out or change this in the iOS Settings app, under Game Center.

Why we use the data, and the legal basis

We use usage statistics to understand how players move through the levels, to tune difficulty and energy, and to improve the game. We use crash reports to find and fix bugs. We do not use the data for advertising or profiling, and we do not try to identify you.

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the game stable and improving it (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). For users in Turkey, the legal basis is the legitimate interest of the data controller under KVKK Article 5(2)(f). The data is pseudonymous and is not used for advertising.

Who processes the data

Google (Google LLC, and Google Ireland Limited for users in Europe) processes the usage statistics and crash reports for us under its Firebase and Google Analytics terms. Google may process the data in the United States and other countries, under its data protection terms for international transfers. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

We do not share the data with anyone else.

How long we keep it

  • Usage statistics: event and user-level data in Google Analytics is kept for 2 months, then deleted automatically. Aggregated reports with no user-level data may be kept longer.
  • Crash reports: kept for 90 days, then deleted.
  • Progress on your device, in the Keychain and in your iCloud: these copies stay after you delete the app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive a copy of it. In the EU and UK you can also complain to your data protection authority. In Turkey you have the rights in Article 11 of the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). We cannot see or delete the copies of your progress in the device Keychain and in your iCloud, and the game cannot delete them either.

To use these rights, email us at the address above.

How to delete your data

  • Delete the app. This resets the installation ID. Your saved progress stays in the Keychain and in your iCloud. Data already sent is deleted automatically after the periods above.
  • Email us. We do not know who you are, so we usually cannot find your records by name or email. Tell us the approximate date you installed the game and your device model, and we will do what we can with the usage statistics and crash reports. We cannot remove the progress copies in the Keychain and in your iCloud. We answer within 30 days.

Children

Tensile is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in their country (between 13 and 16 in the EU). The game has no ads, no chat and no account, and the usage statistics it sends are pseudonymous.

If you are a parent or guardian and think your child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. We only access the Firebase console with a protected account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Languages

The game is available in 13 languages. This policy is available in English and Turkish. If the two differ, the English version applies.
